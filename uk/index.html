Frequently Asked Questions

How does Heatoor work?

A PTC ceramic core starts producing even heat as soon as Heatoor is switched on. A quiet fan moves the warm air around, so the whole room reaches a comfortable temperature in minutes.

Does it need installing or assembling?

No. There's no wiring, no vents and no tools. Plug it into a standard socket, choose your temperature and you're done.

Is it safe with children and pets around?

Yes. It has overheat protection and an automatic shut-off, and the casing stays cool to the touch – ideal for busy family homes.

How big a room can it heat?

Rooms of up to around 50 m² (538 sq ft), depending on insulation and layout – perfect for bedrooms, home offices, lounges, student rooms and flats.

Can it cool in summer too?

Yes! The fan mode moves fresh air around on warmer days, so you get year-round comfort from one compact device.
